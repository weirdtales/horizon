## Format

```text
# ADR-000N: Title

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated

## Context
Why this decision is needed.

## Decision
What was chosen.

## Alternatives
Other options considered.

## Consequences
Tradeoffs and follow-up obligations.
```
